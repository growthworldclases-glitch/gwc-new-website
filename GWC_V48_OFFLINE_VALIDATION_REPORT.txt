GWC V48 OFFLINE VALIDATION REPORT
Source: user-uploaded GWC_V47_JOIN_CLASS_ACCOUNT_FLOW_REPAIR.zip

Meet join changes:
- Student join entry points route through one final V48 handler.
- Resolves the student's assigned course using courseId/requestedCourseId.
- If course.teacherId is explicitly set, only that active assigned teacher's profile Meet URL is accepted. It does not silently open an old course-level URL when that assigned teacher's link is missing.
- Legacy course Meet URL is a fallback only when the course has no explicit teacherId mapping.
- Validates HTTPS meet.google.com URL and standard meeting-code path.
- Opens the normal HTTPS Meet URL directly from the user click; no forced AccountChooser redirect, no attempt to change or store Google credentials.
- Existing non-student join handlers are delegated to their previous handlers.
- Added read-only diagnostic window.gwcV48MeetDiagnostic() for support.

Account persistence limitation:
- GWC student login ID/password is separate from Google sign-in. A website cannot securely choose, save, or force a Google account for Meet without Google's own account/session controls and user authorization.
- Android Meet/Chrome may reuse an already signed-in Google account, but Google can still show account selection/sign-in. V48 does not promise to eliminate that Google-controlled prompt.
- Select the intended Google account in the Meet app/Chrome once and keep that Google account signed in. If the prompt continues, check which accounts are signed into Meet and Chrome; GWC cannot override this.
